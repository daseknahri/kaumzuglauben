# KaumZuGlauben — Brand

**Name:** KaumZuGlauben
**Pitch / tagline:** Unglaubliche Fakten, die wirklich stimmen.
**Byline:** KaumZuGlauben Team
**Domain (planned):** kaumzuglauben.getemoji.site
**Market / locale:** Deutschland · de_DE · Europe/Berlin

## Idea
A German shocking-facts publication built for Facebook traffic: true stories and facts so
astonishing they read as invented — history shocks, nature superpowers, body wonders,
everyday myths fact-checked, and world records with the stories behind them. The brand's
whole trust model is the counterweight to the shock: **every shock backed by a source.**
Loud tabloid energy on the surface (Anton caps, shock red), calm verified reading
underneath (Literata) — never the sibling sites' looks (not Oma Gerda's lilac storybook,
not Deutsche Rezepte's green kitchen).

## Type
Anton (self-hosted woff2) for display headlines — uppercase on cards, hero, tiles and
the brand name; sentence case for the article H1 so long German headlines stay readable.
Literata (self-hosted woff2) for body reading text. Sans (system) for meta/UI only.

## Marks
- Icon: original generated emblem — a bold white exclamation mark centred in a red
  starburst badge on near-black ink (a punctuation device, not text; no logos, no real
  people, no third-party marks).
- Hero: original generated scene — a giant glossy red exclamation mark in a dark studio,
  amber rim light, floating curiosities kept abstract (no signage, no readable text).
- Wordmark: "KAUMZUGLAUBEN" set in Anton with a red exclamation device, composited with
  Pillow over transparent plates — never model-rendered text (playbook §1.6).

## Voice & rules
Staunen, aber wahr — every shock backed by a source. Headlines promise the astonishment
and withhold the twist (the Facebook click has to earn the reveal); the article then
delivers the fact calmly, names its kind of source, and labels anything unproven as a
myth or a single-source claim. Never fabricate numbers, quotes, records or events; what
cannot be verified does not get published. Corrections are prompt and noted in the
article. All imagery is our own created content — no stock, no lifted photos.
